Compliance Alert — April 1, 2026 deadline has passed The transitional grace period for pre-existing contracts under ESSB 5814 expired April 1, 2026. All active client contracts must now collect Washington retail sales tax on advertising services. If you have not done this, take action immediately.
ESSB 5814
Advertising Services Tax
EnactedEffective Oct 1, 2025
Primary impact on your agency. Advertising, media buying, SEM, social, creative, and campaign planning are now subject to retail sales tax (~10.55% in Seattle) and B&O reclassification from Services to Retailing (0.471%). Grace period expired Apr 1, 2026.
Status: Fully in effect — compliance required now
B&O Rate Changes
Service B&O Rate Increase (>$5M)
EnactedEffective Oct 1, 2025
Limited SMB impact. Service & Other Activities B&O rose from 1.5% to 2.1% only for businesses over $5M gross income. Agencies under $5M: no change on non-advertising service B&O. Advertising services shift to Retailing B&O at 0.471%.
Status: In effect — low risk for SMB agency
ESSB 6346
Millionaires' Income Tax (9.9%)
Enacted — Legal Challenge Pending
Effective Jan 1, 2028 (if upheld)
Monitor only. 9.9% flat income tax on household income over $1M. Affects agency owners/partners earning above threshold. Constitutional challenge filed — outcome uncertain.
~570 days until effective date (if upheld)
SB 6346
Small Business Relief Package
EnactedMost provisions: Jan 1, 2029
Future relief — 2029. Repeals sales tax on IT consulting, custom software, data processing, security, temp staffing. Expands small biz B&O credits. Doubles B&O filing threshold to $250K. Does NOT repeal advertising services tax.
~930 days until relief provisions take effect
